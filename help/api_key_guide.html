Your Claude Key

Ten minutes, one website, and about the cost of one coffee a year. This page walks you through the key that turns on Roka's AI features, and what they cost (spoiler: almost nothing).

First, take a breath. An "API key" is just a long password that lets Roka talk to Claude (the AI made by Anthropic) on your behalf. That is the whole thing. You are not signing a contract, installing anything, or subscribing to a monthly plan.

You pay only for what you actually use, and typical Roka use is a few dollars per school year. And if your district or a colleague already gave you a key, your entire setup is one paste. Start there.

What the key turns on

Five things, all inside Roka:

  • Importing sheet music from photos. Point your phone at a cover, and the title, composer, and details fill themselves in.
  • Enriching a piece. Difficulty level, a description, program notes, the details you never had time to type.
  • Reginald. Ask questions about your program in plain English ("who still has a school clarinet?").
  • Importing old concert programs. Feed it a PDF of a past program and your performance history fills in.
  • Seating chart AI Assist. Describe what you want in plain words and it arranges the room.

Everything else in Roka (inventory, checkouts, barcode scanning, repair invoice scanning, uniforms, budgets, seating charts you build yourself) works with no key and no cost at all.

If you were given a key (most people)

  1. Open Roka and click any AI feature.

    Try Reginald, or Import from Photos in Sheet Music. The first time, Roka simply asks for your key, right there.

  2. Paste the key and click Save and continue.

    That is the whole setup. The feature you clicked carries on, and every other AI feature works from then on too. (You can also paste it under Settings, in the LLM Configuration tab, and click Test Connection if you like seeing a green light.)

Getting your own key (only if nobody gave you one)

You will need an email address, a credit or debit card (a personal card is fine, this is a $5 purchase), and about ten minutes.

  1. Go to console.anthropic.com and click Sign Up.

    Use your school email or a personal one, either works. You can sign up with Google to skip making a new password. If it asks whether you want "Claude" or the "Console," you want the Console. (This is separate from the Claude chatbot app, and you do not need a Claude subscription.)

  2. Add credits under Billing.

    In the left menu, find Billing (it may be under Settings). Add your card and buy the smallest amount of credits, $5. This is like putting money on a copy card: it only spends as you use it.

    While you are there, make sure auto-reload is off if you want a hard ceiling on spending. With auto-reload off, the absolute most you can ever be charged is what you chose to put in.

  3. Create the key under API Keys.

    In the left menu, click API Keys, then Create Key. Name it anything ("Roka" is fine) and click create.

  4. Copy the key right away.

    It looks like sk-ant-api03-… and is shown only once. Copy it and paste it somewhere safe for the next two minutes (a sticky note file is fine, you will delete it after). If you lose it, no harm done: delete that key and create a new one.

Then paste it into Roka exactly as in the section above, and delete the sticky note copy.

Sharing one key at your school

Three or four directors at one site can share a single key: one person follows Part 1, and everyone pastes the same key into their own Roka in Part 2. Budget $25 to $50 for the year and you will have money left over. Just remember a shared key means shared credits, so pick one person to own the account.

Common worries, answered

Is this a subscription?
No. There is no monthly fee. You buy credits once and they sit there until used. If you never touch an AI feature, you never spend a cent.
Can it run up my card?
Not if auto-reload is off. The card is only charged when you choose to buy credits. You can also set a monthly spend limit in the Console.
What if my key gets out?
Treat it like a password: do not email it around or post it. If it does leak, log into the Console, delete that key, and make a new one. Takes one minute.
Is student information safe?
Roka sends only what a feature needs to do its job: the photo of the music, the invoice image, or the question you typed (Reginald also sees the current-year roster so it can answer questions about your program). Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API data is not used to train their models.
I heard Claude is free for teachers now. Do I still need this?
Yes, they are two different things. Claude for Teachers (free for verified US K-12 educators who sign up by June 30, 2027) gives you the Claude chat app itself, which is wonderful for lesson planning and worth claiming. But Roka talks to Claude through the API, which is billed separately, and the free teacher account does not include API credits. Get both: the free account for your own chatting, and the $5 key for Roka.
Do I need to be techy for any of this?
If you can set up a Spotify account, you can do this. The scariest part is the name "API key," and you already know it is just a password.

If the district buys one key for everyone

This is the tidiest option, and it can start small with zero risk of overspending. API credits are prepaid: the district puts a fixed amount on the account, and that amount is a hard ceiling, not an estimate. There is no monthly bill and no way to be charged past what was purchased.

Based on a year of real pilot use (about $5 to $10 per active teacher), $500 in prepaid credits comfortably covers roughly 40 music teachers for a school year. The district makes one Anthropic Console account, creates a Workspace per school (each with its own spending cap), and hands each site its own key. The Console shows usage per school in real time, so if the credits ever run low, that is not a budget surprise, it is the evidence for scaling up next year. Teachers then paste the key the first time they click an AI feature, and that is their entire setup.
